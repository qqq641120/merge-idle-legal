Privacy Policy

Orb Merge (working title) · Effective date: November 18, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Orb Merge (the "Game") collects, why, and the choices you have. The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

1. Information we collect

DataSourcePurpose
Anonymous player ID (a random identifier created when you first open the Game; no name, email or login) Supabase (our game backend) Cloud backup of your game progress, server time for offline earnings, recording purchases so rewards are delivered exactly once
Game progress (board, coins, gems, levels, settings related to the game) Supabase Cloud save / restore and preventing cheating
Purchase history (product, store transaction ID, date) Apple App Store / Google Play via RevenueCat, and Supabase Delivering and restoring in-app purchases, preventing duplicate rewards, handling refunds
Advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS if you allow tracking, Android Advertising ID / App Set ID), approximate location derived from IP address, ad interactions, device and diagnostic information Google AdMob Showing ads, measuring ad performance, preventing ad fraud

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

2. Advertising and your choices

  • On iOS, the Game asks for permission before using your advertising identifier for tracking (App Tracking Transparency). You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, or another region where consent is required, a consent form from Google is shown before personalized ads are served. You can change your choice at any time in the Game: Settings → Ad privacy settings.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Purchasing Remove Ads removes interstitial ads.

Google's use of data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Service providers

  • Supabase – game backend and database (privacy policy)
  • RevenueCat – in-app purchase processing and validation (privacy policy)
  • Google AdMob – advertising (privacy policy)
  • Apple App Store / Google Play – payments; we never receive your payment card details.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data retention and deletion

Game progress is kept while you play and for up to 24 months after your last session. Purchase records are kept as long as required for tax, accounting and fraud prevention. To request deletion of your data, open an issue at the contact link below and include the Player ID shown in the Game under Settings. Uninstalling the Game removes the data stored on your device.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. Contact us to exercise these rights.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change and significant changes will be announced in the Game.

7. Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on this site's repository.
