Privacy Policy

Moonbun Orbs · Effective date: November 19, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Moonbun Orbs (the "Game"), made by Moonbun Studio ("we"), collects, why, and the choices you have. The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

1. Information we collect

DataCollected byPurpose

Your email address and anything you write, if you contact us by email (for example via Settings → Report a problem, which also fills in your Player ID, app version and device type) Us (by email) Answering your message and fixing problems

We do not collect your name, contacts, photos or precise location. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Reminder notifications are scheduled on your device by the Game itself; no notification data is sent to us, and you can turn them off in Settings or in your phone's settings.

2. Advertising and your choices

  • On iOS, the Game asks for permission before using your advertising identifier for tracking (App Tracking Transparency). You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, or another region where consent is required, a consent form from Google is shown before personalized ads are served. You can change your choice at any time in the Game: Settings → Ad privacy settings.
  • On Android you can opt out of personalized ads by deleting your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Purchasing Remove Ads, or an active Captain's Club membership, removes interstitial ads.

Google's use of data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Service providers

  • Supabase – game backend and database (privacy policy)
  • RevenueCat – in-app purchase validation and subscription management (privacy policy)
  • Google AdMob – advertising (privacy policy)
  • Google Play / Apple App Store – payments; we never receive your payment card details.

We do not sell your personal information. Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries where they operate, using the safeguards described in their privacy policies.

4. How long we keep data

  • Game data and player ID: kept until you delete it (see below), so your progress is not lost if you take a long break.
  • Purchase records: after you delete your data, we keep a record of each purchase (product, order ID, date) without your player ID, as long as required for refunds, tax, accounting and fraud prevention. This also stops the same purchase from being delivered twice.
  • Technical logs: kept for a limited time by Supabase and then deleted automatically.
  • Emails: kept as long as needed to answer you and follow up.

Data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the Game. On Android, your phone may include the Game's on-device data in your device backup to your Google account; this is controlled by Google and your device settings.

5. Deleting your data

  • In the Game: open Settings → Delete my data. This permanently deletes your game data on your phone and on our server, your player ID and transfer codes, and your customer record at RevenueCat. It needs an internet connection and cannot be undone. One-time purchases such as Remove Ads can be restored later with Restore Purchases; purchased gems cannot.
  • By email: if you no longer have the Game, email pocketcometgamesdev@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and, if you have it, the Player ID shown in the Game under Settings. We will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription. Cancel Captain's Club in your Google Play or App Store subscription settings.

6. Legal basis and your rights

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, we process data to provide the Game you asked for (cloud save, purchases), for our legitimate interests in keeping the Game secure and fair (anti-cheat, logs), to meet legal obligations (purchase records), and with your consent for personalized ads.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Contact us to exercise these rights.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change and significant changes will be announced in the Game.

8. Contact
